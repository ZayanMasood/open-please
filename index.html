Why Open Please

Belonging should not require fitting into someone else’s box.

Many community activities are designed around one “right” way to communicate, play, learn, or socialize. Open Please starts somewhere different: with flexibility, curiosity, and respect for each child’s way of experiencing the world.

We are building a small, sustainable program—not a clinical service—that can host welcoming events with trusted local partners.

Good questions

Before we open the doors.

Is Open Please already a registered nonprofit?

Not yet. Open Please is presented here as a community initiative in development. The first priority is validating the need, building a responsible pilot, and working with established local partners before deciding whether separate nonprofit incorporation is useful.

Is this therapy or medical care?

No. Open Please is planned as a recreational and educational community program. It will not diagnose, treat, or provide individualized medical advice.

Who will help design the program?

Autistic people, parents and caregivers, educators, disability organizations, and community partners should all have meaningful input before a pilot launches.

How will children be kept safe?

A pilot should operate through a qualified host partner with written safeguarding rules, appropriate adult supervision, role-specific volunteer preparation, caregiver consent, and any screening required by the host.
