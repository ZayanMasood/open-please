About Open Please

There should be more places where kids can show up exactly as they are.

Many community activities are built around one expected way to communicate, socialize, learn, or participate. Open Please takes a more flexible approach.

Our goal is to create welcoming activities where children can follow their interests, take breaks, participate differently, and feel comfortable being themselves.

We are starting with community partnerships and family input so the programs grow from real needs.

Our approach

Accessibility should be
part of the plan.

Every child is different. Our activities are designed with flexibility from the beginning.

01

Know what to expect

Visual schedules, simple explanations, and information about the space before an activity.

02

Participation is flexible

Quiet spaces, breaks, sensory supports, and different ways to take part.

03

Listen and improve

Feedback from children, families, volunteers, and partners helps shape future activities.

04

Safety comes first

Activities should include appropriate supervision, caregiver involvement, volunteer preparation, and clear boundaries.

Questions

A few things to know.

Is Open Please a medical program? +

No. Open Please is intended to provide recreational, educational, and community experiences. It is not a medical or therapeutic provider.

Who is Open Please for? +

The initial focus is autistic children and their families in Central Indiana.

Who helps shape the programs? +

Programs should be shaped with meaningful input from autistic people, families, educators, and community organizations.

Can I volunteer? +

Yes. Use the contact form below to start a conversation about volunteering or helping with future activities.
